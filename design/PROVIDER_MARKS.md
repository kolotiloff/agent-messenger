# Provider identification marks

These small marks identify third-party providers supported by Messenger; they are not Messenger branding or an endorsement. Claude belongs to Anthropic, Cursor to Anysphere, and the OpenAI mark used beside Codex belongs to OpenAI. Provider names and session aliases remain visible; unknown senders get a generic icon.

SVG path sources from Simple Icons (CC0 icon collection; trademarks remain with their owners):
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/claude.svg
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/develop/icons/cursor.svg
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/14.15.0/icons/openai.svg
- Collection license: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md

The paths are embedded locally in `internal/localui/assets/app.js`. No third-party image request or telemetry is made at runtime. The official provider guidelines remain authoritative: https://cursor.com/brand, https://openai.com/brand/, https://www.anthropic.com/legal/trademark-guidelines.
